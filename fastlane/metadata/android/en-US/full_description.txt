Sharkord is an open-source, self-hosted communication client designed as an independent alternative to proprietary chat platforms. Connect to your own Sharkord server and take full ownership of your data, voice, and conversations.

Features:
• Real-time Text Chat: Chat in public or private text channels with markdown formatting, mentions, and custom emoji support.
• Media & Attachments: Share images, videos, audio notes, and documents with built-in playback and media previews.
• Low-Latency Voice Channels: Powered by WebRTC and Mediasoup for crisp, low-latency group voice communication.
• Video Calling & Camera Sharing: Join video conversations with multi-user grid views and front/rear camera switching.
• Screen Sharing: Watch high-definition desktop or mobile screen broadcasts, or stream your own screen directly from Android.
• Picture-in-Picture (PiP): Keep watching screen shares and video streams in a floating window while using other apps.
• Background Voice: Stay connected in voice channels while browsing channels or using other applications with background audio service.
• Server & Channel Administration: Manage server settings, roles, permissions, invitations, and channel hierarchies on the go.
• Biometric Security: Secure access to the application using fingerprint or biometric authentication.
• Privacy-Focused: No third-party trackers, no analytics SDKs, and completely decentralized communication under your control.
